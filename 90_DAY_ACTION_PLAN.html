Parent & Child Emotional Wellbeing Workshops for Schools in Beirut — Week-by-Week Execution Roadmap

Window: Weeks 1–13 · Monday 27 July – Sunday 25 October 2026 — timed to the Lebanese school year (start late September/early October). August = asset building; September = outreach peak; October = pilots and first partnership proposals.

Owners: D = Dorina (expertise, delivery, all public-facing approvals) · A = AK (research, asset production, pipeline, tracking, drafts) · D+A = joint.

Standing rule: nothing school-facing — message, post, PDF, or slide — is sent or published without Dorina's explicit approval. This applies every week and is not repeated in each row.

Companion documents: detailed content lives in OFFER_ARCHITECTURE.md (catalog, pricing), SCHOOL_OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md (scripts, tracker spec), EXECUTIVE_PRESENCE_LINKEDIN_X_PR.md (profile copy, content calendar), SCHOOL_PITCH_DECK_OUTLINE.md (deck), BILINGUAL_COPY_BANK.md (approved wording), RISK_ETHICS_COMPLIANCE.md (guardrails — read before Week 1).

1. The 90 Days at a Glance

Phase Weeks Dates Theme Exit gate
Phase 1 — Build 1–5 27 Jul – 30 Aug Assets, identity, target list, content engine live Gate B passed: outreach-ready

Phase 3 — Deliver & convert 10–13 28 Sep – 25 Oct Pilots delivered, proposals presented, first partnership signed Gate D: day-90 review

Approval gates (decision points, not formalities)

Gate When What must be true to pass Decider
Gate A — Identity & positioning End of Week 2 (9 Aug) Dorina has approved: the core positioning lines (EN+AR), her public name format, the credential-placement rule, the visual direction, and the LinkedIn profile copy Dorina
Gate B — Outreach-ready End of Week 5 (30 Aug) One-pager, deck, bio, feedback forms, safeguarding statement, and consent form approved and produced; 30-school list complete with named contacts; ≥10 committed warm introductions; all outreach scripts approved D+A
Gate C — Delivery-ready End of Week 9 (27 Sep) ≥8 discovery meetings held; ≥3 pilots scheduled with dates, rooms, and language mix confirmed; counselor alignment call done (or booked) for each pilot school; consent/logistics checklist sent to each school D+A
Gate D — Day-90 review Week 13 (by 25 Oct) KPI review vs. targets; pricing-validation notes consolidated; testimonials banked; Phase-2 go/no-go on the initiative brand (only after trademark/registry checks) D+A

If a gate fails: do not push forward on the calendar — fix the gate. A week's slip in August is recoverable; skipping Gate B and outreaching with weak assets is not.

4. Corrective Playbook — If the Numbers Lag

Symptom (checkpoint) Trigger Correction
Reply rate < 50% (Week 7 Friday) Warm routes too thin or messages too formal Return to network mapping — request 5 more intros; switch premium first-touch from email to WhatsApp-after-intro; never increase cold volume
< 4 meetings by end of Week 8 Pipeline stalling at "contacted" Counselor-first entry at 3 stuck schools; parent-committee route activated in parallel; one extra free-intro slot for a strategically chosen premium school
< 3 pilots locked at Gate C Meetings not converting Re-check pitch: are we proposing a specific pilot with two dates in every meeting close? Shrink the ask (60-min talk instead of 90); offer online-delivery option
Pilot NPS < 8.5 Content or language-mix mismatch Debrief with counselor; adjust language model and examples per segment (OFFER_ARCHITECTURE.md §6); rehearse before next pilot
No proposal trending to yes by Week 11 Budget timing or trust gap Lead with term package (bridge), not annual; scholarship-seat framing; extend window — a November signature is a win, the date is not sacred
Security/instability disruption Any week Pause promotional touches (warm greetings only); invoke online fallback for booked sessions; shift content to supportive pillar-E posts; resume when appropriate

5. Weekly Operating Rhythm

  • Monday (30 min, A): queue the week's content for approval; update tracker next-actions; flag the week's 3 priority accounts.
  • Daily (20 min, D): LinkedIn ritual — replies, 3–5 substantive comments, 5–6 connection requests (presence doc §3.2).
  • Within 24h always (D+A): post-meeting confirmations; within 48h: post-pilot summaries and proposals.
  • Friday (30 min, D+A): pipeline review ritual (playbook §7.2) + KPI log + next week's top 3 + content review. This meeting is the heartbeat of the plan — it does not get skipped, even in delivery weeks.

All dates assume a 27 July 2026 start. All prices referenced are working USD ranges to validate in the first 10 school conversations. All school-facing materials require Dorina's approval before use. Guardrails in RISK_ETHICS_COMPLIANCE.md apply to every action in this plan.

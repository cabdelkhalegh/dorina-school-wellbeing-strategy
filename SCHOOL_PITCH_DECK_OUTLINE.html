Parent & Child Emotional Wellbeing Workshops for Schools in Beirut — Slide-by-Slide Pitch Deck

What this is: the complete structure, headline copy, and speaker notes for Dorina's school-facing deck — ready to build in Canva or PowerPoint using LOGO_BRAND_KIT.md. Copy blocks marked EN/AR have approved Arabic equivalents in BILINGUAL_COPY_BANK.md.

Two versions from one master:
- Discovery Deck (slides 1–11 + 14): for the 45-minute discovery meeting. No pricing on screen — pricing is discussed live, and only after discovery (see slide-12 rule).
- Proposal Deck (all 14 slides + tailored slide 12): for the debrief/proposal meeting after a pilot, personalized with the school's own feedback data.

Compliance (applies to every slide): never lead with hypnotherapy — credentials appear only on slide 8, exactly worded (Master in Clinical Hypnotherapy · Master in NLP / Coaching · Master in Time Line Therapy). No clinical, diagnostic, or treatment claims anywhere. No children's faces without documented consent. All prices are working USD ranges to validate; never print a single fixed number.

The Deck, Slide by Slide

Slide 1 — Title

  • Headline (EN): Parent & Child Emotional Wellbeing Workshops for [School Name]
  • AR: ورش عمل الصحة النفسية والتوازن العاطفي للأهل والأطفال — [اسم المدرسة]
  • Subline: Practical, bilingual support for your parents, your students, and your team · 2026–27
  • Visual: Warm Sand background, seedling/embracing-curve motif in Deep Teal, Dorina's name and title bottom-left. Always insert the school's name — a generic title slide reads like a mass mailing.
  • Premium variant headline: School–Family Emotional Wellbeing Program for [School Name].
  • Speaker notes: Thank them for the time; one genuine, specific compliment about the school. Frame the meeting: "I'd love to spend most of our time hearing about your families — I'll keep the slides short." In discovery meetings, this deck follows the 45-minute agenda in SCHOOL_OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md §5 — discovery questions come before slide 4.

Slide 2 — The Moment Our Families Are In

  • Headline (EN): Beirut's parents are asking for help. Schools are where they ask.
  • AR: أهالي بيروت يطلبون الدعم — والمدرسة هي المكان الذي يثقون به.
  • Body (3 lines max): 72% of caregivers in Lebanon report their children anxious or nervous; 62% sad or depressed (UNICEF, 2025) · National Mental Health Strategy 2024–2030 calls for school-linked wellbeing support · Parents increasingly expect schools to lead here.
  • Visual: one large stat (72%) in Deep Teal, source cited small; no distressing imagery.
  • Speaker notes: Deliver with hope, never alarm: "This number isn't meant to worry you — you see it in your corridors already. It means every school that gives parents practical support is meeting a real, felt need." Bridge immediately to agency. Never use fear as a lever; that violates the brand and reads as manipulation to seasoned principals.
  • Segment adaptation: NGO deck adds MEHE/UNESCO MHPSS program reference (alignment with funder language); community deck softens the stats into one line and leads with "what parents ask us every day."

Slide 3 — What Parents Ask (the felt problem)

  • Headline (EN): "How do I get him to talk to me?" — what we hear from parents everywhere
  • AR: «كيف أجعل ابني يتكلم معي؟» — أسئلة نسمعها من كل الأهالي
  • Body: 5–6 real, anonymized parent questions as floating quote cards: homework battles · school-morning anxiety · screen conflicts · the child who "keeps it all inside" · discipline without daily war · confidence and comparison.
  • Visual: quote cards in Soft White on Warm Sand; mix AR and EN quotes deliberately — the bilingualism is the message.
  • Speaker notes: Pause here. Ask: "Which of these do you hear most from your parents?" Their answer tells you which track to emphasize on slides 5–7. Write their words down — verbatim phrases become the proposal language.

Slide 4 — What I Do

  • Headline (EN): Practical, bilingual wellbeing support for your whole school community
  • AR: دعم عملي وثنائي اللغة لكل المجتمع المدرسي
  • Body — the positioning line verbatim: "I help schools and families strengthen children's emotional wellbeing through practical parent–child support, coaching-based tools, and bilingual workshops adapted to each school community."
  • Three-track diagram: Parents · Children · School Partnership — three circles feeding one center: the child's wellbeing.
  • Speaker notes: One breath per track, no detail yet: "Parents get tools they use the same evening. Children build confidence in small, safe groups. And for schools that want more than events, we build a year-round program together." Flag the key differentiators now: bilingual by design, practical not theoretical, and — say it explicitly — "coaching-based and non-clinical: I complement your counseling team, never replace it."

Slide 5 — Track 1: Parent Workshops

  • Headline (EN): Parents leave with tools they use tonight
  • AR: أدوات يستخدمها الأهل من الليلة نفسها
  • Body: 2-hour interactive workshops, up to 40 parents, AR/EN. Catalog titles (show 4–5, tuned to slide-3 answers): Talk So They Listen · Calm Starts at Home · Firm and Kind · Beside My Anxious Child · School Stress: A Family Toolkit.
  • Visual: workshop-title cards with AR titles beneath; one consented room photo if available.
  • Speaker notes: Walk through ONE workshop in 60 seconds (pick the one matching their pain) — audience, one concrete tool from it, and the take-home sheet. Mention format credibility: "Every session is interactive — pair practice, live demonstrations, printed bilingual tool sheet. Parents don't sit through a lecture."

Slide 6 — Track 2: Child Support Workshops

  • Headline (EN): Small groups. Big skills. Your staff always in the room.
  • AR: مجموعات صغيرة، مهارات كبيرة — وبحضور فريق مدرستكم دائماً.
  • Body: Series of 4–6 sessions, groups of 8–15, activity-based, by age band (6–9 / 10–12 / 13–15). Sample series: The Confidence Club · My Feelings in Colors · Calm and Brave · Focus Power. Safeguarding banner across the bottom: School staff present in every session · Written parental consent before session 1 · Non-clinical, skills-based · Concerns referred to your counselor.
  • Speaker notes: Lead with the safeguarding banner, not the catalog: "Before the content — three rules I never break: your staff are in the room for every child session, every child has written parental consent, and anything that needs more than a skills workshop goes straight to your counselor." This slide converts skeptical principals; the rules ARE the pitch.

Slide 7 — Track 3: The School Partnership (flagship)

  • Headline (EN): Not an event. A calendar.
  • AR: ليست فعالية واحدة، بل برنامج على مدار السنة.
  • Body: A co-designed October–June wellbeing calendar: monthly parent workshops · child workshop series by age band · teacher mini-sessions each term · awareness campaigns · per-term feedback reports to leadership.
  • Visual: a miniature 9-month calendar strip (from OFFER_ARCHITECTURE.md §4 sample) with icons per month — the single most persuasive visual in the deck.
  • Speaker notes: "One evening helps parents for a week. A calendar changes how a school community talks about feelings for a year." For premium schools add the enrollment angle: "Partner schools present this as their school–family wellbeing program — it becomes part of how the school describes itself to prospective families." Do not discuss price here; if asked, bridge: "There are three partnership levels — let me show you how schools choose, and we'll talk investment once we've defined what fits [School]."

Slide 8 — Who I Am

  • Headline (EN): Dorina [Last Name] — Parent & Child Emotional Wellbeing, Beirut
  • AR: دورينا [الشهرة] — الصحة النفسية والتوازن العاطفي للأهل والأطفال، بيروت
  • Body: Professional portrait (warm, per brand kit) · bilingual facilitator, Beirut-based · "My training includes: Master in Clinical Hypnotherapy · Master in NLP / Coaching · Master in Time Line Therapy" · "In schools, my work is coaching-based and educational — non-clinical, preventive, and delivered in coordination with your counseling team."
  • Speaker notes: This is the ONLY slide where credentials appear, in exactly this wording and this order-of-information: role → approach → training → scope boundary. If hypnotherapy questions come, use the approved response in SCHOOL_OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md §4.9 verbatim: school programs contain no hypnotherapy and no therapy of any kind; the training deepens understanding; session outlines are shared in advance. Offer the certificate verification pack if they wish — volunteering verifiability builds more trust than the certificates themselves.

Slide 9 — How I Work With Your Team

  • Headline (EN): Alongside your counselor. Never instead.
  • AR: إلى جانب المرشد(ة) في مدرستكم — لا بديلاً عنه(ا).
  • Body — four commitments: Content shared with your counselor before any session · Staff present in all child workshops · Clear referral pathway: your counselor first; licensed professionals when needed (incl. Embrace Lifeline 1564 on all parent handouts) · Aligned with your safeguarding policy and the spirit of Law 422.
  • Speaker notes: If the counselor is in the room, address this slide to them directly and ask for their input on content. If not: "Before anything runs, I'd like your counselor's eyes on the content — could you introduce us?" This sentence wins deals. The subtext of the whole slide: she makes our counselor's job easier and our risk smaller.

Slide 10 — What a Session Feels Like (proof)

  • Headline (EN): What parents say when the forms come back
  • AR: ماذا يقول الأهل بعد الورشة؟
  • Body: feedback statistics from delivered sessions ("average value rating 9.2/10 · 87% said they'd attend monthly") · two short parent quotes (consented, name-optional) · one school-staff testimonial when available.
  • Visual: big NPS number, quote cards, consented room photo.
  • Speaker notes: Before the first pilots exist, this slide shows the measurement system instead: the bilingual feedback form, what gets reported to leadership, and the promise — "You'll never have to wonder whether it worked; your leadership gets the numbers and your parents' own words after every session." Replace with real data after pilot #1 — this slide is why every pilot must produce feedback forms.

Slide 11 — Where We Start: The Introductory Session

  • Headline (EN): Start with one evening. Judge everything from that.
  • AR: نبدأ بلقاء واحد — واحكموا بأنفسكم.
  • Body: Raising Confident Kids in Stressful Times — a 60–90 minute evening for your parents · fully prepared, bilingual, with take-home tool sheet and promotion kit · feedback summary delivered to your leadership within 48 hours.
  • Speaker notes: The close of the discovery meeting. Offer per segment policy: premium — "I'd offer [School] this first session with my compliments — I'm selecting a small number of Beirut schools for the launch series"; community — symbolic fee. Always propose two concrete dates in this breath and never leave without a date or a named date-decision step (playbook §5.4 close script).

Slide 12 — Partnership Options & Investment (Proposal Deck only)

  • Headline (EN): Three ways to work together in 2026–27
  • AR: ثلاث صيغ للتعاون خلال العام الدراسي
  • Body — three columns (Essentials / Growth / Flagship) with 4–5 inclusion bullets each (from OFFER_ARCHITECTURE.md §4 inclusion table) and the segment-appropriate annual range from the approved pricing logic. Footnote: Investment ranges for 2026–27; termly invoicing available; final scope co-designed with your team.
  • Speaker notes: Present Growth first, always (anchor), Essentials as the accessible fallback, Flagship as the ambition tier. Personalize with the school's own pilot data: "68% of your parents said they'd attend monthly — that's what Growth is built for." Negotiation guardrails apply (playbook §6.5): scholarship seats instead of discounts; trade scope, not rate; pilot fee credited against a signed annual package. In the Discovery Deck this slide is omitted — showing prices before discovery anchors the conversation on cost instead of fit.

Slide 13 — Your School's Year, Sketched (Proposal Deck only)

  • Headline (EN): What [School Name]'s wellbeing year could look like
  • AR: هكذا يمكن أن تبدو سنة مدرستكم
  • Body: a draft Oct–Jun calendar personalized with the topics their parents voted for on the pilot feedback forms, their term dates, and their school name in the title.
  • Speaker notes: This is the emotional close of the proposal meeting — the school sees itself already inside the program. Build it from the sample Growth calendar (OFFER_ARCHITECTURE.md §4) with their feedback data slotted in. Invite co-authorship: "This is a sketch — the real version we build with your counselor."

Slide 14 — Next Steps

  • Headline (EN): Three small steps this month
  • AR: ثلاث خطوات بسيطة هذا الشهر
  • Body: 1. A 20-minute call between me and your counselor · 2. [Discovery: introductory parent session on Date A or Date B / Proposal: your chosen option confirmed by (date)] · 3. We announce it to your parents together (invitation kit provided).
  • Contact block: Dorina [Last Name] · WhatsApp · email · LinkedIn · one-pager QR code.
  • Speaker notes: Recap agreements out loud, confirm who sends what by when, ask for the counselor's contact, and book the next touchpoint before leaving. Leave printed one-pagers (never the full deck — the deck is the meeting; the one-pager is the artifact that circulates).

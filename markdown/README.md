## Non-negotiables (apply to every document and everything derived from them)

- **Never lead with hypnotherapy** in school-facing or first-line copy. Lead with parenting, emotional wellbeing, coaching, resilience, child confidence, and school–family partnership. Credentials appear later, as credibility.
- **Credentials, exact wording:** Master in Clinical Hypnotherapy · Master in NLP / Coaching · Master in Time Line Therapy.
- **Non-clinical scope:** no diagnosis, treatment, or medical claims; Dorina's work complements — never replaces — school counselors and licensed professionals. Referral protocol in RISK_ETHICS_COMPLIANCE.md.
- **Child protection first:** staff present in child sessions, parental consent, photo consent, trauma-sensitive language.
- **All prices** are working USD ranges to validate in the first ~10 school conversations. **All name candidates** need Lebanese trademark/commercial-registry checks before use.
